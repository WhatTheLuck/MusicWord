# FluidR3 General MIDI samples

The eight banks in `samples/` use pitch samples from the
[MIDI.js Soundfonts FluidR3_GM MP3 renders](https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/),
published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The original Fluid (R3) SoundFont is by Frank Wen, copyright (c) 2000–2002, 2008.

MusicWord selected every fourth semitone from MIDI pitch 24 to 96,
repackaged those MP3 samples, and transposes them in Web Audio for playback
between the sampled pitches.
